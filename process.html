Process

Our branding process is built to create clarity, differentiation, and long-term brand value not just visual aesthetics.

01

Discovery & Strategy

We begin by understanding the business, market, and audience. This stage includes stakeholder input, brand audit, competitor analysis, and market context review. Based on insights from discovery, we define the strategic foundation of the brand: brand purpose & vision, positioning and differentiation, target audience definition, brand personality & tone of voice, and core messaging.

Outcome A clear brand strategy that guides all creative decisions.

02

Logo Design

We translate strategy into a creative direction that defines the logo design three different logo concepts reflecting brand positioning, crafting a distinctive and memorable logo that embodies the brand, with different logo lockups.

Outcome Approved logo design ready for print and digital use.

03

Visual Language

We develop the full visual identity system, which may include color palette, typography system, graphic elements & iconography, brand imagery style, and pattern.

Outcome A cohesive and distinctive visual identity.

04

Brand Applications

We apply the identity across key brand touchpoints to ensure consistency and usability this may include stationery, digital assets, marketing materials, packaging, or social media templates.

Outcome Brand applications ready for print and use.

05

Brand Guidelines

We prepare a document with the brand system in clear guidelines to ensure consistent use across all channels and teams.

Outcome A comprehensive brand guideline document.
